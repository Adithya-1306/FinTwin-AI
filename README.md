# FinTwin AI

### Your financial decisions should fit your life — not just the market.

FinTwin AI is an AI-powered personal financial twin designed to help users understand how their financial profile, risk tolerance, goals, portfolio, and market conditions interact before making financial decisions.

The project was developed as a hackathon prototype and was later enhanced with market-data integration, stock analysis, scenario simulation, and explainable financial decision support.

> **Disclaimer:** FinTwin AI is an educational/hackathon prototype and does not provide professional financial advice or execute real brokerage trades.

---

## 🚀 What is FinTwin AI?

Traditional investment platforms often focus heavily on market conditions and individual assets.

FinTwin AI takes a different approach by first understanding the **person behind the investment**.

The system builds a lightweight financial profile using factors such as:

- Income
- Monthly expenses
- Savings
- Debt
- Dependents
- Risk tolerance
- Investment horizon
- Financial goals
- Existing portfolio

It then uses this profile to generate personalized financial insights and simulate different scenarios.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Financial Health Score

Generates an explainable financial health score based on multiple aspects of the user's financial profile.

### 🧬 Personal Financial Twin

Creates a digital representation of the user's financial situation and allows the user to update their financial information.

### 📈 Market & Stock Analysis

The enhanced version introduces market-data integration and stock-level analysis.

The system considers multiple factors including:

- Valuation
- Earnings
- Technical indicators
- Market/news signals
- Volatility
- Portfolio concentration
- User risk tolerance

### 🔮 What-if Simulator

Users can explore hypothetical scenarios such as:

- Market crashes
- Changes in monthly investment
- Temporary job-loss periods
- Changes in financial conditions

The simulator shows how these scenarios could affect the user's financial position.

### 🤖 AI Decision Engine

Combines the user's financial profile with market and portfolio factors to generate an explainable decision signal.

The same stock can produce different results depending on the user's:

- Risk tolerance
- Portfolio concentration
- Financial situation
- Investment horizon

### 🎯 Life Goals

Users can define financial goals and track them as part of their overall financial profile.

### 🕒 Decision Timeline

Provides an explainable timeline of financial decisions and recommendations generated by the system.
